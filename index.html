Your questions, answered

Everything people ask before they join

I have no tech background and I'm not good with computers. Can I still learn?

Yes. These programmes are designed for beginners. You don't need a tech degree, a coding background or any previous experience. We start from the foundations and teach step by step.

If you can use a smartphone or laptop and you're willing to practise, you can learn.

What will I be able to do after the training?

You'll be able to apply your chosen skill on real projects. For example, after Data Analysis you should be able to collect, clean and organise data, create professional Excel reports, build Power BI and Tableau dashboards, write SQL queries, use Python for analysis and explain your findings clearly.

Every course includes practical projects you can add to your portfolio and show to employers or clients.

Can I really learn online? I heard physical classes are better.

Yes. And online is good preparation for how modern work is changing.

Tech skills are practical. You become good by attending live sessions, practising with real datasets, completing projects, getting feedback and building a portfolio. Our programme gives you live evening classes, recorded lessons, assignments, trainer support and a learning community.

Online learning also builds the habits remote and international employers look for: working independently, communicating clearly online, managing your time, using digital tools and delivering results without being supervised in person.

Physical classes suit people who prefer face-to-face interaction, but traffic, transport costs, distance, work and family commitments make it hard for many people to keep going. Online, you learn from home, replay difficult lessons and keep going even when life gets busy.

What exactly does the 1-month internship involve?

The internship is practical and project-based. You apply what you've learned to real-style tasks in your field, such as cleaning data, preparing reports, building dashboards, analysing business data and presenting insights.

At the end you'll have practical work for your portfolio, CV and LinkedIn profile.

What does "remote job placement support" mean? Does it guarantee a job?

It means we help you become ready for opportunities: CV and LinkedIn guidance, portfolio review, interview preparation, career advice, and sharing relevant job, internship and freelance openings.

We don't promise an automatic job, because employment also depends on your skills, projects, consistency and how actively you apply. What we promise is strong preparation and support to help you compete.

What certificate will I receive, and who issues it?

You'll receive a digital Certificate of Completion issued by Miramore Institute, under its parent organisation Miramore Consulting Limited. It is CPD-certified and accredited by the CPD Standards Office and CPD UK.

After you complete the programme it's sent to your email. You can download it, print it, add it to your CV and share it on LinkedIn. Each certificate has a verification link and barcode so employers or clients can confirm it's genuine.

CPD stands for Continuing Professional Development: structured learning that helps people build, update and strengthen workplace skills throughout their careers.

Are the classes truly online? What if I miss one?

Yes, classes are 100% online on Zoom, so you can join from anywhere in Nigeria. They hold on Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays from 9:00 to 10:30pm, so students, workers and busy people can attend.

Every live class is recorded. As soon as class ends, the recording is sent to the class group and to you personally, so you can catch up straight away.

What do I need to join?

A laptop (any specification is fine), data or internet access, and a Zoom account. If you don't have a Zoom account, we'll set one up for you free.

How much time do I need each week?

Plan for the live classes plus personal practice. A realistic commitment is about 6 to 10 hours a week, including classes, reviewing recordings, assignments and practice. Consistent practice matters more than rushing.

Why is the early fee ₦30,000 instead of ₦50,000? Is the quality different?

No, it's exactly the same training. The ₦30,000 early price is a limited-time reward for people who register early. After 4 November the fee is ₦50,000. The curriculum, live classes, certificate, internship and career support stay the same.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. Pay ₦15,000 first to secure your slot, then pay the balance after your first month of classes, before 4 December. On the early price, that is ₦15,000 now and ₦15,000 later.

What if I can't continue after I start?

We don't offer refunds, but you can pause your studies and return later to continue the class when you're ready.

Which course should I choose?

If you like numbers, reports and problem-solving, look at Data Analysis, Data Science or Machine Learning. If you like creating things, look at UI/UX, Graphic Design or Web Development. If you want to protect systems, choose Cyber Security & Ethical Hacking. For business and finance, consider Accounting Software or Sales & Digital Marketing.

Not sure? Tell us about yourself on WhatsApp and we'll recommend one.

What happens after the training ends?

You stay part of the Miramore learning community and keep receiving guidance on projects, career development and opportunities shared with the community. The goal isn't just to finish a class, but to keep growing after the cohort ends.
